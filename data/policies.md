# Store policy knowledge base (source: supplied internal manual, version 2.1, June 2025)

## Identity and scope (manual sections 1, 1.2, 7.1)
Empório da Música sells musical instruments only, not accessories such as strings, picks, cables, cases, pedals, or amplifiers. Address: Rua 14 de Maio, 3200 — Centro, Campo Grande - MS, 79202-333. Phone printed in company details: (67) 3341-4444. Mission tagline: “Sua música começa aqui.”

## Hours (section 2)
Monday-Friday 09:00-18:00; Saturday 09:00-13:00; Sundays and holidays closed. Special dates may have extended hours; check official store communications.

## Payment (section 3)
PIX cash payment: 5% off list price. Debit accepted. Credit up to 12 interest-free installments subject to minimum installment: 1-3 installments have no minimum except installment below R$50; 4-6 installments minimum R$80; 7-12 installments minimum R$100. Bank slip is paid upfront and may take up to 3 business days to clear. Combining payment methods is allowed for purchases above R$2,000. Discounts do not stack: PIX discount does not apply to promotional prices.

## Returns and exchanges (section 4)
Online regret return: request within 7 calendar days after receipt, no reason required; original packaging, unused, with accessories/manuals. Store pays return shipping; refund in original payment method within 10 business days. Manufacturing defect exchanges may be requested within 30 calendar days after purchase; after that, contact manufacturer warranty (store may mediate). Preference exchanges within 7 days, subject to availability, perfect condition and original packaging; price difference charged/refunded. Exceptions: customized/setup-adjusted instruments, explicitly final-sale liquidation items, and wind-instrument mouthpieces for hygiene.

## Shipping (section 5)
Greater Campo Grande: free over R$500; otherwise flat R$35; estimated 1-3 business days. Other cities: Correios PAC 5-12 business days, SEDEX 2-5, Jadlog 3-8; price calculated from destination ZIP, weight, and dimensions. Large instruments may need an individual quote. Shipments include insurance. If visibly damaged, refuse delivery and contact the store immediately. Tracking is sent after dispatch.

## Promotions (section 6)
Promotions are not cumulative and depend on stock. Always show original price, discount percentage, and promotional price. An expired promotion must not be promised; use current system status.

## Service behavior (section 7)
Friendly, professional, concise, music-aware. During opening hours, target response time is within five minutes; after hours, state when service resumes. Never provide price, stock, or delivery timing without querying current system data. For unavailable products, be transparent and suggest available alternatives. Complaints should be acknowledged empathetically and escalated; expected return within 24 business hours. When unsure about a policy, ask store management.

## Warranty and privacy (sections 8-9)
Manual states 90 calendar days legal warranty for manufacturing defects from receipt; manufacturer warranty varies (typically 6 months to 2 years), see product certificate. Do not claim coverage for misuse, falls, extreme climate exposure, unauthorized modifications/repairs, natural wear, or non-functional cosmetic damage. Personal data is used for order processing, status, consented marketing, and legal obligations; not shared for third-party marketing. Deletion requests can be made via store contact channels.

## Contact inconsistency
Manual lists two different WhatsApp numbers ((67) 3341-4444 in company details and (67) 3321-4500 in section 7) and inconsistent email spelling. Do not select one as authoritative; offer the physical address and ask a human representative to confirm contact details.
